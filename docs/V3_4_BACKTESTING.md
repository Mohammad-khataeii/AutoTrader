# V3.4 backtesting

`Backtester.run_async` uses MarketScanner, DecisionEngine, RiskEngine, PaperExecutionGateway, PositionManager and the financial repository. It creates an isolated replay database; it does not write simulated historical fills into the live paper account. The synchronous wrapper uses the same implementation.

Clock injection restricts indicators to completed observations. An entry based on a completed candle executes at the next observed bar opening. Spread, fees, slippage, liquidity participation, partial IOC fills, gap stops, conservative stop/target collisions, cooldown, regime veto and equity circuit breakers pass through the runtime components.

Reports include net equity P&L, completed-position win/loss statistics, profit factor, expectancy, drawdown, Sharpe/Sortino, exposure, notional turnover, dominant entry-plan strategy contribution, entry regime and explicit assumptions/provenance. Open positions at the end are disclosed. Losing replays are labelled UNDERPERFORMED_INITIAL_CASH.

`walk_forward` uses rolling training history as indicator warmup and disjoint test windows with fixed parameters; no optimization or future leakage is represented. Multiple assets are supported. The smallest supplied native timeframe drives execution; four completed contiguous primary bars form the same higher-timeframe confirmation used by the runtime. Future observations are excluded. Overlapping signals are attributed to the dominant entry-plan strategy; this is not a causal signal decomposition. Entry policy is selectable as MARKET or LIMIT IOC through the engine, CLI, API and Backtesting Lab. Limit IOC uses the observed ask as its maximum price; insufficient executable liquidity/price produces no fill and releases the reservation through the same gateway. It does not invent a resting queue or queue priority. A qualifying replay verifies market fills and zero economic effect for unfilled limits.

CLI: `autotrader-backtest --candles path/to/historical.json`. Supplying historical data is mandatory; provenance is retained. Add `--entry-order-type LIMIT` for explicit limit IOC replay. The previous generated-price CLI example and its disconnected broker mathematics were removed.
